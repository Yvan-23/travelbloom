About Us

Our Company

TravelBloom was founded in 2020 with one mission: to make travel planning simple, inspiring, and accessible. We curate recommendations for beaches, temples, and destinations around the world so you can spend less time searching and more time exploring.

Our Values

  • Authentic, well-researched recommendations
  • Respect for local cultures and the environment
  • Honest, friendly customer support

Guidance for Team Members

  1. Verify every destination's details before adding it to the database.
  2. Use high-quality, properly licensed images.
  3. Respond to customer inquiries within 24 hours.
  4. Keep descriptions concise, accurate, and welcoming.
  5. Share ideas and feedback in the weekly team meeting.
